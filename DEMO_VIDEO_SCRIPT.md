# Recording the demo video (3–4 minutes, clear audio)

Use a laptop with headphones plus a phone on a different network, so you hear the real call. Record the laptop screen with the laptop mic and also play the call on speaker, or use a second device to record the phone side. Tools: OBS, Loom, or Windows Game Bar (Win+G).

1. 10 s: say what the app is and show the live site.
2. 60 s: **Web to phone number.** Place call → 📞 Phone number → your mobile. Pick up, speak both ways, show the consent step, the live checklist, hang up, wrap-up, Save to CRM.
3. 60 s: **AI to phone number.** 🤖 AI → phone. Answer, say yes to recording, talk to Avani (ask the price), show the live transcript, hang up.
4. 20 s: show the web-to-web calls (human and AI) work too.
5. 20 s: say how it works (Twilio + Gemini, keys only on the server).
